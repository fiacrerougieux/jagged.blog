In [Taking Jaggedness Seriously](https://helentoner.substack.com/p/taking-jaggedness-seriously), Helen Toner says that models get good first at tasks a computer can check, because that is what they can be trained on. Every check here is either measured by a script or decided by my eye. So we now have a script to count and see if it agrees with that thesis.

Across all 327 runs, measured checks passed 82% of the time and eye checks 72% so that checks.

<Verdict>
  Note: On the eight tasks that have both kinds, it flips: measured checks passed 64% and eye checks 78%. But that is a small sample so need to have more diverse tasks.
</Verdict>
