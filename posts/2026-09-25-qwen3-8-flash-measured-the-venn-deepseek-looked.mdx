## Checks

<Checks task="tikz-coffee-venn" />

The sorting is the easy half. An iced latte has espresso, milk and ice, so it goes in the middle, and a pour-over has none of them, so it goes outside. Both
models got all eight drinks right every time. The hard half is fitting the words in, because the overlaps are small lens shapes and "iced long black" is
wide. DeepSeek put labels across the circle lines in two of its three runs, and Qwen worked out where each label would land in Python first and got it right every time.
