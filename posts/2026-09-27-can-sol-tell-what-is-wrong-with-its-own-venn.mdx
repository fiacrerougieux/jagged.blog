This is the last of four posts on whether specifying to measure helps model avoid overlapping text and lines. Here, with 10 runs instead of 3 for Sol. I had the plain prompt telling Sol to make a Venn diagram, another prompt to make a Venn and check that the text does not overlap with the lines and another to check its own render to ensure no overlap.

| Labels clear of the lines | Sol, ten runs |
|---|---|
| Plain prompt | 9 of 10 |
| Told the list | 9 of 10 |
| Told to check its own render | 9 of 10 |

<Verdict n="10 runs a prompt" p="1.0, Fisher exact, committed before the runs">
  No indication of difference between prompt, but of course, to measure this statistically, I would need a very large sample. So really, we cannot tell yet.
</Verdict>
