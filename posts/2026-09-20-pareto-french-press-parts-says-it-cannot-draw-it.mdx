<Finding>
  Pareto drew the filter stack upside down. But ask it in plain words and it
  gives the correct order, calling the cross-plate "the bottom support plate",
  which is the exact fact its own drawing contradicts.
</Finding>

[Both models in the duel](/posts/2026-09-18-pareto-vs-sol-french-press-both-inverted-the-stack)
inverted the stack. So do they not know the order, or do they know it and fail
to draw it?

<Prompt task="svg-french-press" version="probe v1" text="List the parts of a French press plunger from top to bottom." />

Pareto gave the right order in words, Sol gave it upside down, and DeepSeek 4.1 Flash got it half right but is the only one that drew it correctly.

<Verdict n="1 run a model">
  In one run Pareto stated the filter order correctly for a tenth of a cent,
  and in one run it drew that order upside down for a quarter of a dollar. So
  the knowledge question still needs asking properly.
</Verdict>
