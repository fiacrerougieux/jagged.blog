## What's jagged here

Five models have done this task now, and four of them drew a filter that would
not hold grounds back. DeepSeek 4.1 Flash got it right for four cents, and that
is the number Bonsai was meant to beat.

So did compression take the knowledge? Bonsai's reasoning names the parts and
argues about the gaps between them, so the knowledge is still there. What went
is the step from planning to looking. It plans one geometry, writes another and
stops.
