I gave each model these two pictures and asked: "Name everything wrong with it, most serious first. Say if you think nothing is wrong.” Both press have nothing wrong with them.

| Says the filter is upside down | Opus's press | Pareto's press |
|---|---|---|
| Opus 5.5 | 3 of 5 | 2 of 5 |
| Pareto | 0 of 5 | 1 of 5 |

<Verdict n="5 runs a model on each press">
  So about half the time, Opus thinks the filter is upside down. This is like when it draws it, about half the time it draws it upside down.
  Pareto, on the other hand, almost always (9 out of 10) does not think that the filter is upside down, and it is right.
</Verdict>

Looking back at when it drew the french press, everytime it drew it updside down, it also said it upside down. So it is consistent between its words and its drawing. But it is just that about half the time, it has a different belief about the order of the filter stack.
