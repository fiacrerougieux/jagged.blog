I tested many models to see whether I could get statistical significance where a model knows the order of a filter stack but cannot draw it.
For most models I tested we cannot say much, but the Opus 5.5 runs somewhat suggest that it knows the order but often cannot draw it in order.

<Checks task="svg-french-press" model="anthropic/claude-opus-5.5" label="Opus 5.5" />

All seven models, as passed of decided on the order:

| | In words | Drawn |
|---|---|---|
| Opus 5.5 | 5 of 5 | 2 of 5 |
| Sonnet 5.5 | 5 of 5 | 2 of 3 |
| GPT-5.6 Sol | 5 of 5 | 2 of 3 |
| DeepSeek 4.1 Flash | 3 of 3, all after searching the web | 1 of 1 |
| GLM 5.3 FlashX | 0 of 4 | 0 of 2 |
| Qwen3.8 27B | 0 of 3 | 0 of 2 |
| Pareto | 0 of 2 | 2 of 3 |

<Verdict n="5 runs an arm for Opus, 1 to 5 decided for the others" p="0.17 for Opus, and 0.37 for all seven without the web runs, Fisher exact, committed before the runs">
  With p at 0.17, the Opus 5.5 seems to indicate that, in general, it knows the order but cannot draw it in order, which is interesting.
</Verdict>
