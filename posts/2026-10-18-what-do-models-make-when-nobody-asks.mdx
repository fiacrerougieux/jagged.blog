## Nothing

Gemini 4 Argon made nothing. All three runs went quiet mid-reply and OpenRouter cut them off.

The checker only tests whether poking the page changes the picture more than leaving it alone. It is no good here. Opus's tides and crabs move on their own, and random clicks miss the Moon, so it fails.
