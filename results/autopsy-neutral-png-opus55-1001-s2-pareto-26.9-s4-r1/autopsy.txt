Yes, as a schematic of a conventional French press.

The main parts and their assembly order are consistent:
- The knob attaches to the top of the plunger rod, which slides through
  the lid's central opening.
- Below the rod, the filter stack is ordered correctly: spring/spiral
  plate on top, fine mesh screen in the middle, and cross/support plate
  underneath. The spring-loaded rim holds the mesh against the beaker
  wall, helping keep grounds from passing around its edge.
- The lower fastening holds the filter assembly together on the rod.
- The glass beaker sits in the outer frame, which carries the handle.

This is an exploded view: the gaps between parts are for illustration,
not gaps that remain in the assembled press. The filter operates inside
the beaker, with the lid seated at the top.

Two minor qualifications: the fastening arrangement varies by model
(some cross plates thread directly onto the rod), and the "3-piece
filter" bracket should cover parts 4-6 only, not the separate nut at 7.
Neither changes the basic assembly shown.
