The filter stack (parts 4-6) is drawn in the wrong order.

On a standard French press plunger (Bodum-style), the order from the rod
downward is:

  cross plate  ->  mesh filter screen  ->  spiral plate  ->  retaining nut

The cross plate with its spokes sits on top. It threads onto the rod and
backs the mesh. The spiral plate, whose spring-coil rim seals against the
glass, sits at the bottom of the stack, and the retaining nut screws up
against it. When you turn a real plunger over, the spiral plate and the nut
are the parts you see.

The diagram flips this. It puts the spiral plate (4) on top and the cross
plate (6) right above the nut (7). If you assembled it this way, the
spoked cross plate would face the coffee grounds and the spiral plate
would not be clamped by the nut.

Fix: swap parts 4 and 6, so the order is 4 Cross plate, 5 Mesh, 6 Spiral
plate, 7 Retaining nut. Then change the note on the cross plate to say it
"threads onto rod, backs the mesh".

The rest looks right: knob on top of the rod, rod through the lid bore,
rod threaded at both ends, all parts on one axis, and the beaker drops into
the frame. The beaker spout and the lid's pour opening are both on the
right, opposite the handle on the left, which is correct.
