In this knitting chart of a cup cosy with a coffee-bean motif, the bean comes out 2.4 times as long as it is wide once the fabric is knitted, where it should be between 1.3 and 1.8 times. Change the bean so it knits true. The stitches are drawn wider than the rows, so the motif needs rows added or stitches taken away. Change nothing else: the grid, the cell size, the key and the rest of the chart stay as they are.
